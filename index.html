How it works

No retainers. No lock-in. Just the hours you need.

Most agencies and consultants sign you up to a fixed monthly fee and a minimum term, whether that month's work justifies it or not. This runs differently.

The usual way
  • Fixed monthly retainer
  • Locked into a 3, 6 or 12-month contract
  • Same fee whether it's a busy month or a quiet one
  • A bundled scope of services, take it or leave it
Working with Walk in the Park Digital
  • Billed by the hour, nothing more
  • No minimum term — step away whenever it suits you
  • Pay only for the hours actually worked
  • Choose exactly which services you need

That flexibility runs the full width of what's on offer: full-stack support across paid, organic and analytics, run end to end — or a handful of hours for a single consultation, a campaign revamp, or a second opinion on something that isn't quite working. The scope fits the job, not the other way round.

What we can help with

No fixed packages — just tell us what's going on

People tend to come to us for one of a few reasons. None of them are a fixed scope — they're just the kind of thing that tends to need an hour, or ten, depending on what's actually going on.

Data & tracking

"Something's not adding up"

GA4 that was never set up properly, conversions that don't look right, or reporting nobody quite trusts. Usually a few hours to find what's broken and fix it — sometimes a rolling few hours a month once it's in good shape, to keep it that way.

Audit

"Are my ads/SEO actually working?"

An outside, experienced look at what's running — paid search, paid social, programmatic, or organic — and whether it's doing what it should. Often just a couple of hours for a proper audit and a clear set of next steps.

Hands-on

"I need someone to actually run this"

Hands-on management of a channel — search, social, display, SEO, whatever it is — for as long as it needs running. Could be a few hours a week, could scale up around a launch or a busy period, then scale back down.

Second opinion

"Just want a sanity check"

Sometimes it's not a project, just an hour or two to sanity-check a plan, a quote from another agency, or a decision before it's made.

However much of this you need, and however often — two hours this month, twenty the next — that's the arrangement. Nothing's fixed except the hourly rate.
